Cupoftea.social is a friendly corner of the fediverse, and this is its Android app. Log in to your Cupoftea.social account and keep up with people across the fediverse in a chronological timeline. No algorithms, ads, or clickbait.

In the app you can:

READ AND EXPLORE

■ Keep up with the people you care about in a chronological feed
■ Follow hashtags to keep up with specific topics
■ Discover new people and see what is happening

POST

■ Post to your followers or the whole world, with polls, images and videos
■ Put posts behind content warnings
■ Take part in conversations with other people

CURATE

■ Create lists of people so you never miss a post
■ Filter words or phrases to control what you see
■ Get notified when a specific person posts with the bell button

AND MORE

■ A theme that adapts to your colour scheme, light or dark
■ Share and scan QR codes to quickly exchange profiles
■ Log in and switch between multiple accounts

Cupoftea.social is part of the fediverse, so you can follow and talk to people on other servers too, using the same account.
