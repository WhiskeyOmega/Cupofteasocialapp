First Launched in 2022. This is the app for the Mastodon Community of Cupoftea.social. A Wholesome & Positive social network that puts the human being back in social networks. Described as a breath of fresh air by some.

Cupoftea.social is a instance of Mastodon, this app is a reskin of the Mastodon App with customized features specifically designed for it.

Cupoftea.social is not affiliated with Mastodon GmbH

EXPLORE

■ Discover new writers, journalists, artists, photographers, scientists, and more
■ See what’s happening in the world

READ

■ Keep up with people you care about in a chronological feed with no interruptions
■ Follow hashtags to keep up with specific topics in real time

CREATE

■ Post to your followers or the whole world, with polls, and high quality images and videos
■ Participate in interesting conversations with other people

CURATE

■ Create lists of people to never miss a post
■ Filter words or phrases to control what you do and don’t want to see

AND MORE!

■ A beautiful theme that adapts to your personalised colour scheme, light or dark
■ Share and scan QR codes to quickly exchange Cupoftea.social & Mastodon profiles with others
■ Login and switch between multiple accounts
■ Get notified when a specific person posts with the bell button
■ No spoilers! You can put your posts behind content warnings
